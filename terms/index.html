Using Ummi

By creating an account you agree to these terms and our Privacy Policy. You must be 18 or over and keep your sign-in details to yourself.

Health information

Ummi gives general information and estimates, not diagnosis or treatment. Expert answers are general guidance. In an emergency call 112 or go to the nearest hospital.

Community Guidelines

Circles are a safe space. We have zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive members. Do not post:

  • harassment, bullying, hate or sexual content;
  • medical advice that could cause harm, or selling medicines;
  • spam, advertising, links or phone numbers;
  • anything that identifies another person without her permission.

If someone may be at risk of harming herself, report the post so we can help.

Reporting and moderation

Every post and reply has Report and Hide member. Our team reviews reports within 24 hours, removes content that breaks these rules and removes members who post it. Posts reported by several members are hidden automatically until reviewed.

Your content

You own what you post. You let us show it to members of the circle you posted in. You can delete your posts at any time.

Your account

You can delete your account at any time from Me. We may suspend accounts that break these terms.
